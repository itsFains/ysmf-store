The brand

Built beyond
merch.

itsFains is where the personality started. YSMF is where the clothing goes next: a brand designed to stand on its own whether somebody knows the stream or not.

Clothing first.
Story behind it.

YSMF grew from the energy around itsFains and the phrase You Sexy Motherfuckers, but the goal was never to stop at creator merch.

Signature keeps that original personality visible — louder graphics, stronger contrast and the attitude tied to the streams. Minimal takes the same confidence and translates it into cleaner pieces that can work every day.

The collections can look different, but the idea underneath them stays the same: wear something because it feels like you, and wear it like you mean it.
